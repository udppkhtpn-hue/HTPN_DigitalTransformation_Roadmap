leaders (Microsoft, Grab, Shopee) at the Executive Digital Leadership Programme.

Permaisuri Norashikin (HTPN), Kajang, Ministry of Health Malaysia.

20 sheets · 50 clicker presses · planned 20:25 against a 5:00 slot

## Ground rules for this deck

- Every figure below is quoted from the source deck. Nothing is estimated or invented.
- All copy and figures live in one file (`src/content/scenes.ts`); the screens read
  from it and hold no copy of their own.
- `steps` is how many clicker presses happen inside a sheet. Scenes render purely
  from the step number, so going backwards mid-talk works.
- Deliberately not used: a news screenshot carrying the Prime Minister's photograph.
  S-03 typesets that headline and pins the cut-out article beside it instead.

## Look, in one paragraph

"Blueprint": the hospital is drawn as an architectural sheet while he speaks. Dark
navy ground, cyan line work, amber for achieved results and the human voice, red
only for the dengue figure. Numbers arrive as dimensions on a drawing rather than
stat cards. Each sheet's name stands large at the top-left; a title block
bottom-right carries the sheet number instead of a progress bar. One
orchestrated animation per sheet; everything else holds still.
